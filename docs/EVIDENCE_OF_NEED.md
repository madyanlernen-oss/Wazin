## 4. Background Literature & Academic Evidence / الدراسات المرجعية

The problem addressed by Wazan—work-life conflict, chronic overwork, and psychological strain—is documented in international and regional public health literature:

1. **WHO/ILO Joint Estimates on Long Working Hours (2021):**
   - *Finding:* In 2016, approximately 745,000 deaths from ischaemic heart disease and stroke globally were attributable to working long hours ($\ge 55$ hours/week). Working $\ge 55$ hours/week was associated with an estimated 17% higher risk of dying from ischaemic heart disease (RR 1.17) and a 35% higher risk of dying from stroke (RR 1.35) compared to working standard hours (35–40 hours/week).
   - *Caveat:* Global epidemiological estimates based on systematic reviews and meta-analyses; observational and attributable burden estimates.
   - *Source:* [WHO/ILO Global Burden of Disease attributable to long working hours Q&A (2021)](https://www.who.int/news-room/questions-and-answers/item/global-regional-and-national-burdens-of-ischemic-heart-disease-and-stroke-attributable-to-exposure-to-long-working-hours-for-194-countries-2000-2016)

2. **Malebari et al. — Frontiers in Public Health (2024):**
   - *Finding:* In a cross-sectional study of $n = 728$ university students in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia, screening revealed depressive symptoms in 81.5% and anxiety symptoms in 63.6% of participants.
   - *Caveat:* Conducted using PHQ-9 and GAD-7 screening questionnaires, which indicate symptom prevalence rather than clinical psychiatric diagnoses. Cross-sectional design limits causal inference.
   - *Citation:* Malebari et al., *Frontiers in Public Health*, 2024. [doi:10.3389/fpubh.2024.1441695](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2024.1441695)

3. **Albasheer et al. — Medicine (Baltimore) (2025):**
   - *Finding:* In a cross-sectional study among $n = 390$ medical students in Jazan, Saudi Arabia, 79.5% reported a preference for self-managing emotional and psychological problems rather than seeking formal help. Reported barriers included lack of time (61.5%) and concerns regarding financial cost (57.9%).
   - *Caveat:* Single-institution sample of medical students; self-reported survey responses.
   - *Citation:* Albasheer et al., *Medicine (Baltimore)*, 2025; 104(6):e41362. [PMC11709213](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11709213/)

4. **Borgmann, Rattay, Lampert — Frontiers in Public Health (2019):**
   - *Finding:* A scoping review examining 25 studies on the relationship between work-family conflict (WFC) and subjective/physical health outcomes found consistent evidence that chronic conflict between work and family roles correlates with diminished self-rated health and elevated strain.
   - *Caveat:* The authors noted that associations varied across genders and directions (work-to-family vs. family-to-work conflict), and the majority of included studies were cross-sectional rather than longitudinal.
   - *Citation:* Borgmann L, Rattay P, Lampert T. Health-Related Consequences of Work-Family Conflict from a European Perspective: Results of a Scoping Review. *Frontiers in Public Health*, 2019; 7:189. [PMC6629821](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6629821/)

## 5. Synthesis: How Literature Supports the Problem / ربط الأدبيات بالمشكلة

The literature reviewed demonstrates a well-established **association**—not direct singular causation—between chronic domain imbalance (excessive working hours, work-family friction, and academic role overload) and adverse health and psychological outcomes. Regional data from Saudi Arabia highlight that individuals facing stress frequently encounter time and cost barriers, with a pronounced preference for self-guided management and early personal intervention. 

Wazan addresses this documented context not by offering clinical healthcare or therapy, but by providing an accessible, private, local-first platform for self-reflection across 8 balanced life dimensions. By helping users proactively identify single-domain bottlenecks and commit to micro-habits in Eisenhower Quadrant 2 (Important & Not Urgent), the platform aims to support users in managing everyday life tensions before they escalate into acute strain.
